Who I Am

Passionate About Science & Maths

I am Deep Rijal, a dedicated Science and Mathematics Teacher from Nijgadh, Bara, Nepal. With over 10 years of classroom experience across two schools, I specialise in making General Science, Chemistry and Physics engaging and approachable for every student.

My teaching philosophy is built on positive encouragement and reinforcement — motivating students through interactive instruction, real-world observation, and structured lesson plans aligned to curriculum standards. I work hard to identify each student's challenges and resolve them early.
